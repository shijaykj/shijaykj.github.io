02 / Approach

How I think about performance

Generating load is the easy part. The value is in asking the right questions of the data.

Principle 01

Averages hide the pain

An average response time can look healthy while one user in twenty waits four times longer. I judge releases on percentiles and the shape of the distribution, not a single mean.

p95 = —

Principle 02

Realistic workloads beat big numbers

A thousand users hammering one endpoint proves little. I model business-critical journeys, think times, data variety and arrival patterns from production behaviour.

Principle 03

Every bottleneck is a queue

Latency climbs when work arrives faster than a resource can serve it. Little's Law (L = λ·W) tells me where to look: threads, connection pools, locks, CPU or I/O.

Principle 04

Baselines turn data into decisions

A number on its own means nothing. Comparing every build against an agreed baseline makes regressions visible and gives teams a clear go / no-go signal.
